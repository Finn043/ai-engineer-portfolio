Building AI on dependable foundations

Data modeling, retrieval, and practical machine learning.

I am an early-career AI Engineer in Melbourne. I am most interested in the work that makes an AI feature useful beyond a prototype: preparing dependable data, retrieving relevant evidence, and making outputs clear enough to inspect.

My experience spans analytics data modeling for a chatbot at MoMo, support for an insurance RAG initiative at CoverGo, and data analysis and pipeline work at Filum.ai.

Start with the data and evidence

AI systems inherit the strengths and weaknesses of the information around them. A chatbot needs data that can be analyzed consistently; a RAG assistant needs relevant source passages and citations that people can check.

I enjoy working on these foundations because they connect model behavior to the real information a product is meant to use.

How I approach applied AI

01

01 · Ground answers in sources

In my BJJ research assistant, PDF pages are extracted, divided into overlapping chunks, embedded, and retrieved by cosine similarity before an LLM responds with file and page citations.

02

02 · Build the data layer

At MoMo, I built the analytics data model for an AI chatbot project. That experience keeps me attentive to how event data is structured for meaningful analysis.

03

03 · Learn the model mechanics

My personal projects implement K-Means, PCA, linear regression, loss functions, and gradient descent with NumPy, so I can inspect how the algorithms behave rather than treating them as black boxes.

What I am building toward

I am completing formal study in machine learning alongside projects that put the concepts into working systems. I try to be careful about what has been measured, what remains an assumption, and where a model needs stronger evaluation.

I bring a data analyst's attention to source quality and stakeholder context, with growing hands-on experience in Python, embeddings, retrieval, FastAPI, and model fundamentals.
